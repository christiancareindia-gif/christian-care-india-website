ABOUT US

Compassion in action

Christian Care India is focused on helping poor and vulnerable Christian children by responding to practical needs that affect their wellbeing and future.

Our work centers on nutritious food, education, medicine and treatment. We believe care is not only something we speak about — it is something we put into practice through service, kindness and responsible stewardship.

OUR WORK

Where support can make a difference

Food support

Helping children access nutritious food and basic nourishment.

Education

Supporting learning and educational needs so children can keep moving forward.

Medicine & treatment

Helping with medicines and treatment needs when children face health-related difficulties.
